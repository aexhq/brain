# ADR-010: Bound journal growth and measure the complete execution path

- Decision date: 2026-08-28
- Status: Accepted
- Compiled: 2026-09-05

## Context

Writing an entire growing context on every decision or turn makes persisted bytes quadratic. Early concurrency measurements also exposed a global worker lock and admissions counted as successful turns; a slow writer could accumulate an unbounded backlog.

## Decision

Remove repeated full-context persistence, keep ordered deltas, surface writer failures, and measure successful end-to-end work rather than accepted or refused requests. Test journal growth across both model calls and turns. Include worker memory and actual concurrency when assessing the runtime.

## Alternatives considered

Moving one full-context write from every decision to every turn reduced a constant factor without fixing growth across turns. Raising worker permits could not fix a mutex held for the entire activation. Database substitution alone did not address either problem.

## Consequences

The original per-session rewritten-state-file optimization was replaced by [ADR-014: Rebuild session views from recorded history](2026-08-30-03-journal-projections.md) and [ADR-032: Use one canonical journal and commit before exposing records or effects](2026-09-05-01-canonical-journal.md); [ADR-019: Record transcript changes as common-prefix deltas](2026-09-02-02-prefix-deltas.md) is the current transcript rule. Specific queue limits and memory/throughput thresholds from old spikes are not current contracts. [ADR-039: Keep performance claims historical until representative baselines are rebuilt](2026-09-05-08-benchmark-policy.md) records which checks remain mandatory.
