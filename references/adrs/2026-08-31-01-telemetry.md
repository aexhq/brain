# ADR-015: Keep telemetry bounded and best effort, and use cursors for durable consumers

- Decision date: 2026-08-31
- Status: Accepted
- Compiled: 2026-09-05

## Context

A telemetry sink can be slow or unavailable. Blocking the turn on observability creates an external availability dependency; an unbounded queue turns that failure into memory exhaustion.

## Decision

Deliver operational telemetry asynchronously through a bounded queue and ordered batches with a limited retry window. Drops are observable when the channel has room. Consumers requiring durable forwarding use committed session Events with saved sequence cursors and own their destination queue and deduplication.

## Alternatives considered

An unbounded observer queue is not durability. A built-in external event bridge would expand Brain into a delivery product. Making all telemetry durable duplicates the canonical record and changes its latency contract.

## Consequences

Telemetry can be lost and a retried batch can be duplicated. Its retry mechanism does not retry model or Tool effects. Actionable failures and awaited extension `emit` use committed Events under [ADR-035: Send each effect once and expose failures to the Agentloop](2026-09-05-04-send-once.md), not this best-effort path. Per-session live feeds and historical reads follow [ADR-037: Release execution at turn boundaries and prepare artifacts before creation](2026-09-05-06-ephemeral.md).
